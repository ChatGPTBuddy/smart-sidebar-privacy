Privacy Policy

Smart Sidebar (AITOPIA) – Chrome extension
Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Chrome extension Smart Sidebar (AITOPIA) (the “Extension”), published on the Chrome Web Store by ChatGPT Buddy (“we”, “us”), handles your information.

1. What the Extension does

The Extension adds an AI assistant sidebar to your browser. It lets you chat with AI models, summarize or translate web pages, analyze files you upload, and get AI answers next to search results. The AI features are provided by our technology partner AITOPIA INC (aitopia.ai).

2. Information that is processed

  • Content you submit – prompts, messages, selected text, page content you ask the Extension to summarize or translate, and files or images you upload. This content is sent to the AI service only when you actively use a feature, so that a response can be generated.
  • Account information – if you sign in (for example with Google), your name, email address and profile picture are processed by AITOPIA to manage your account.
  • Payment information – paid plans are handled by a payment processor (Stripe). We do not receive or store your card details.
  • Settings – your preferences (such as language, theme and sidebar settings) are stored in your browser using Chrome storage.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to provide and improve the Extension’s features: generating AI responses, keeping you signed in, processing subscriptions and remembering your settings.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use or transfer your data for purposes unrelated to the Extension’s core functionality.
  • We do not use or transfer your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • We do not read or collect your browsing history; page content is only processed when you explicitly ask the Extension to act on it.

5. Third parties

To provide the service, data is processed by:

  • AITOPIA INC – AI service and accounts. See the AITOPIA Privacy Policy.
  • AI model providers (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, Meta and DeepSeek) – the content of your requests is passed on to the model you select in order to generate a response.
  • Stripe – payment processing. See the Stripe Privacy Policy.

6. Data retention and deletion

Settings stored in your browser are removed when you uninstall the Extension. To request access to or deletion of account data, contact us at the address below and we will forward your request where needed.

7. Children

The Extension is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date above shows when it was last changed.
